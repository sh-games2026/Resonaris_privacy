Privacy Policy

Resonaris - Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

Resonaris is a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game handles and how that information is used.

We do not sell personal information. We do not require an account to play Resonaris.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Resonaris does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, address, or other account information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as:

  • settings, including sound, haptics, tutorial, and visual preferences;
  • game statistics, including scores, attempts, and accuracy results;
  • saved in-progress game state when the game is paused or restored.

This information is used to provide the game experience and is stored on your device. Removing the app may remove this locally stored information.

Advertising

Resonaris may show banner ads and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional and may be used to receive an in-game reward.

Ads may be provided by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google may collect or process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics according to Google's own policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: Google Partner Sites Policy.

How Information Is Used

Locally stored game information is used to:

  • save and restore your game progress;
  • remember your settings;
  • show your gameplay statistics;
  • support normal game functionality.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Locally stored gameplay data is not shared by us. Advertising partners may process ad-related information as described in their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Resonaris is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

Security

We use reasonable measures to keep the game functioning safely. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but Resonaris is designed to avoid requiring personal account data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the app listing or the project page where this policy is published.
